Viva University — Travel Management

From functional to exceptional

In this session you will sharpen the skills that separate a passing itinerary from an outstanding one. You will review a sample submission, identify what is missing, then apply that same critical eye to your own work — with AI as your grading partner.

The scenario

You work for Chelsey Olsen, CMO at Twilex. She is attending the SaaS Summit in Denver from February 20–23 and wants to visit partner Rocket Labs on the 21st or 22nd. She flies out on the 7th and returns on the 10th. Budget: $3,800 USD. You must book flights (premium economy or business), hotel near the convention center, ground transport, summit registration, and the Rocket Labs meeting — then send her a clear summary email.

Summary email to Chelsey

Hi Chelsey,

I hope this message finds you well. I wanted to reach out to let you know that I have been working diligently on your travel arrangements for the upcoming SaaS Summit in Denver, Colorado and I believe I have put together a solid plan that I would like to share with you in detail.

For your outbound journey, I have secured a premium economy seat on United Airlines flight UA 1842, which departs from San Francisco International Airport at 8:15 in the morning on February 7th and arrives in Denver at 11:40 AM. For your return, you will be on United Airlines flight UA 1901, departing Denver at 2:00 PM on February 10th and arriving back in San Francisco at 3:45 PM. Your hotel is the Courtyard by Marriott Denver Downtown at approximately $180 per night for three nights. The summit registration has also been arranged at a cost of $800 for general admission. Regarding the Rocket Labs visit, I will follow up once a date is confirmed. For transportation during your stay, I would suggest using Uber or Lyft for convenience. The total estimated cost for all arrangements comes to approximately $2,700, which is within your $3,800 budget. Please let me know if you would like any changes or have any questions about the itinerary.

Best regards
